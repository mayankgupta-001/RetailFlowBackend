# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in RetailFlow a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect different opinions, backgrounds, and experiences.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information without permission, trolling, deliberate disruption, and any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The repository maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting unacceptable behavior

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed in the GitHub repository profile or through GitHub's private reporting tools. Do not create a public issue for sensitive reports.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and, where appropriate, kept confidential.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.
